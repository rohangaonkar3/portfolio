My Projects

Here are some of the projects I have developed while building my skills in software development, web development and Artificial Intelligence.

ProxiCart – An E-commerce Website

A web application where users can browse products, filter by category, search items, view product details and add products to the cart.

The application also helps users find products in nearby shops, check shop opening and closing timings, and discover newly introduced products. Shop owners can update stock and timings.

  • Product browsing
  • Category filtering
  • Product search
  • Shopping cart
  • Nearby shop discovery
  • Shop timing information

Billify – Electricity Bill Predictor

A web-based tool that estimates electricity bills based on appliance type, power consumption and usage hours.

Users can enter multiple appliances and the system calculates total electricity consumption and estimated cost in real time.

  • Appliance input
  • Power consumption calculation
  • Usage-hour calculation
  • Real-time bill estimation
  • Dynamic user interface

SafeAI – AI-Powered Crowd Safety

An AI-based crowd safety concept designed to help predict potentially dangerous crowd conditions and support safer movement during large gatherings.

The project focuses on using AI, location information and crowd-related data to identify risk areas and provide safety guidance.

  • AI-based crowd safety
  • Risk-area identification
  • Crowd monitoring
  • Location-based safety information
  • Safer route guidance
